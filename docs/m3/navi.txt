What the emulator's navigation and Pleos signals sent to Downshift (DriveHost adapters), 2026-11-03

## NaviHelper (NaviEvents, tag DownshiftNavi)
- Connects at app start once the safety note is agreed to: "navi connected".
- onCurrentRoadSpeedLimit: 0 only, and repeated. An adb limit (drive --es event limit) therefore stands on its own
  until adb sets 0; otherwise the navi's next 0 would wipe it.
- Route requested with `probe.sh route` from the probe tab (Seoul Station from Pangyo):
    tbt TURN_RIGHT 700.0 대왕판교IC 방면 (14 ahead)
    turn RIGHT city 700.0m (TURN_RIGHT)
  TBTInfo.distance is in metres. The car does not move along the route on the emulator, so the turn stays 700 m away;
  cancel the route before scripted drives (`drive --es event route --es value cancel`), or a demo drive of 700 m
  would judge that stale turn.
- onCameraAlert / onCautionPointInfo: nothing in 20 s with a route on (as in M0). Demo drives inject them.

## Pleos Vehicle SDK (PleosVehicle, tag DownshiftPleos)
- Turn signal: console BlinkLeft / Off arrive as "signal LEFT" / "signal OFF" within about 0.1 s.
- Odometer: the console odometer, moved by ConsoleDrive every 0.1 km of the demo car, arrives through
  registerDistanceDriven (also re-sent unchanged every ~4 s). A 0.20 km trip read 0.20 km from the odometer.
- Seats: every seat reads the opposite of the console's own state. With the console's `seat.occupied` all false
  (driver included), getCurrentSeatOccupancy said ROW_1_RIGHT, ROW_2_LEFT, ROW_2_CENTER and ROW_2_RIGHT were occupied;
  seating the passenger read false, leaving read true. M0 took this for a lagging callback; it is an inversion, and
  re-reading does not change it. PleosVehicle flips seat values while the Virtual Console is connected and trusts the
  SDK otherwise (real car unverified).
